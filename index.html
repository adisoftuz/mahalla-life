Maxfiylik siyosati / Privacy Policy

Oxirgi yangilanish / Last updated: 2026-10-31

O'zbekcha

Mahalla Life o'yini (uz.adisoft.mahallalife) foydalanuvchidan hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaydi va uni hech kimga yubormaydi.

  • O'yin saqlanishi. Pul, mashina, vazifalar, ovoz va grafika sozlamalari faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi. Ular serverga yuborilmaydi. Ilovani o'chirsangiz, ular ham o'chadi.
  • Internet. O'yin internetsiz ishlaydi va tarmoqqa ma'lumot yubormaydi.
  • Ruxsatlar. O'yin kamera, mikrofon, joylashuv, kontaktlar yoki fayllarga kirishni so'ramaydi.
  • Reklama va tahlil. O'yinda reklama, tahlil xizmatlari va uchinchi tomon kuzatuvchilari yo'q.
  • Bolalar. O'yin bolalardan ham ma'lumot to'plamaydi.

Bu siyosat o'zgarsa, shu sahifa yangilanadi. Aloqa: https://github.com/adisoftuz/mahalla-life/issues

English

The Mahalla Life game (uz.adisoft.mahallalife) does not collect any personal data and does not send it to anyone.

  • Saved games. Money, cars, missions, and sound and graphics settings are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to a server, and uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Internet. The game works offline and sends no data over the network.
  • Permissions. The game does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access.
  • Ads and analytics. The game has no ads, analytics or third-party trackers.
  • Children. The game collects no data from children either.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Contact: https://github.com/adisoftuz/mahalla-life/issues
